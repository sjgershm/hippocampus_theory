Histogram of per-dimension widths (sigma_1 and sigma_2) aggregated over all neurons for each of the 8 sessions analyzed in our study (we use the Pfeiffer and Foster, 2013 dataset). Saved the data as a dict, keyed by the session id's ({0, ..., 7}), where each value is just the histogram of widths for that session.

Some notes about this data: The inference method obtains posterior distributions over each sigma_1, sigma_2 for each subfield of each neuron (we assume a weighted mixture of Gaussian tuned "subfields" for each neuron to obtain the overall receptive field). We do a bit of processing to transform those per-width posterior distributions into what I'm sharing now. First, we obtain the MAP estimate from each posterior distribution for each width of each subfield. The histogram I'm sending is obtained by re-sampling a distribution with the MAP widths, weighted by the inverse sum of the posterior variances for each subfield (weight = 1/(var(post. sigma_1) + var(post. sigma_2))). This is how we incorporate uncertainty into our estimates of the MAP widths, and thereby the distribution of widths. High level, the way to think of these histograms is as samples from the distribution of tuning widths for each session (estimated through the certainty-reweighting described above), rather than as histograms of the raw inferred widths.
